## Summary

Pop Off! is a casual offline game. We designed it so that **we do not collect personal data** from you through the App.

## Information we do not collect

The App does **not**:

- Require an account or sign-in  
- Collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts  
- Use advertising identifiers or third-party ad networks  
- Use analytics or tracking SDKs  
- Sell or share personal information with third parties  
- Access your location, photos, microphone, or camera  

## Information stored on your device

The App may store **game progress** on your device only, including:

- Levels you have unlocked  
- Levels you have completed  

This information is saved locally using standard Apple device storage (for example, UserDefaults). It stays on your iPhone unless you back up or sync your device through Apple’s own services (such as iCloud Backup), which are governed by Apple’s privacy practices—not ours.

We do not receive a copy of this progress data on our servers. The App does not operate a Longest Chariot LLC backend for gameplay data.

## Internet use

Gameplay does **not** require an internet connection.

If you open optional links from the App (for example, this privacy policy on GitHub, or an email to support), those experiences are provided by third parties (such as GitHub or your email provider) and are subject to their own privacy policies.

## Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect personal information, we do not seek parental consent for data collection through the App.

## Data security

Because progress is stored only on your device, protecting that data depends on your device security settings (passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, OS updates) and Apple’s platform security.

## Your choices

- You can delete local progress by deleting the App from your device.  
- You can stop using the App at any time.  
- Device-level privacy controls are available in iOS Settings.

## Third-party services

Aside from optional outbound links you choose to open, the App does not integrate third-party analytics, ads, or social SDKs.
