## Common questions

### How do I play?

Every balloon looks the same until you pop it. Find all the hidden prizes for the level — donuts, stars, diamonds, hearts, then crowns — to unlock the next level.

### Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app will remove your progress.

### Does Pop & Seek need the internet?

No. You can play fully offline. Opening optional links (privacy policy, this support page, or email) uses the internet.

### How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email [support@longestchariot.com](mailto:support@longestchariot.com) with details. Screenshots help a lot.

We don’t collect personal data through the App. Read our full policy: [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
